Apple Pie

A tasty apple pie with a flaky crust and perfectly cooked apples. Cosmic crisp apples are the best to use!

Are you team chewy brownies? Then this is the recipe for you. It uses a combination of oil and butter; the oil gives the chewy texture, while the butter provides the rich mouthfeel and taste we love. (Using light brown sugar in addition to granulated sugar also adds to the chewiness and offers a touch of caramel flavor.) Seek out the best-quality unsweetened chocolate you can find for these brownies—chocolate is the main flavor, so it really makes a difference to the finished dessert. There's only one difficult part to this recipe—waiting for the brownies to cool completely before digging in.

Ingredients

  • Ingredients
    • 7 tablespoons unsalted butter, room temperature, plus more for baking pan
    • ¾ cup plus 2 tablespoons unbleached all-purpose flour
    • ¼ teaspoon baking powder
    • ½ teaspoon coarse salt
    • 7 ounces unsweetened chocolate, finely chopped (1 ⅓ cups)
    • 3 tablespoons safflower or coconut oil
    • 1 cup granulated sugar
    • 1 cup packed light-brown sugar
    • 3 large eggs, room temperature

Directions

  1. Preheat oven and prep pan
    • Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-inch square baking pan. Line with parchment, leaving a 2-inch overhang on two sides; butter parchment.
  2. Combine dry ingredients; melt chocolate, oil, and butter
    • In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. In a heatproof bowl set over a pot of simmering water, melt chocolate and butter with oil. Remove from heat.
  3. Add sugars, then eggs; stir in dry ingredients
    • Add both sugars and whisk 10 seconds. Add eggs and whisk vigorously until glossy and smooth, 45 seconds. Using a rubber spatula, stir in dry ingredients. Pour batter evenly into prepared pan, smoothing top with spatula.
  4. Finish mixing dough
    • Drizzle 2 TBS ice water over the flour-butter mixture, and blend. Repeat with an additional 2 TBS water. At this point, you may have to add more water: When a handful of dough squeezed together just holds its shape, you've added enough; if the dough crumbles, continue adding water, 1 TBS at a time, checking the consistency after each additional TBS
  5. Bake and cool
    • Bake until set and a tester inserted in center comes out with moist crumbs, 35 to 40 minutes. Cool in pan on a wire rack 20 minutes, then remove using parchment. Let cool completely on rack before cutting into squares.
