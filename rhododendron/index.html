Pacific Rhododendron

Common alternate names of the plant

    Rhododendron californicum
    Rhodies

Range of physical size for the plant

    This plant grows 3 to 15 feet (1 to 5 m) in height.

Where can this plant be found?

    This plant grows from British Columbia to California. The plant grows west of the Cascades crest and at the coast in Washington. It grows in in fairly dry open forests and edges. It commonly grows along roadsides in the rainshadow of the Olympic Mountains.

Climates where the plant grows best

    It tolerates dry, but prefers damp soil. Rhododendrons thrive in temperate, cool, and humid climates.
