My First Quilt

Welcome to your first step toward making your very first quilt! My name is Audrey, and I know exactly how it feels to be a beginner because I was one too. I'm a 20-year-old student from Washington State who has been sewing since childhood. I started out with simple pillows and gradually worked my way up to full-sized quilts. My dad and aunt taught me everything I know, and I feel so lucky to have had people in my life who could guide me through the process. But I also know that not everyone has family members who sew, and that's exactly why I built this website. Consider me your quilting best friend!

My quilting journey hasn't always been easy, there were plenty of wonky seams, miscounted fabric cuts, and moments where I wondered if I'd ever get it right. But every mistake taught me something new, and looking back, those imperfect early projects are some of my favorites because of what they represent. Quilting has given me a creative outlet, a way to connect with the people who taught me, and a skill I'll carry with me for the rest of my life. I want that for you too, and I genuinely believe that anyone, at any age and any skill level, can learn to quilt with a little patience and the right guidance.

So if you're feeling nervous or unsure where to begin, just know that every single quilter you admire started exactly where you are right now. You don't need fancy equipment, a huge budget, or a family full of sewers, you just need a little curiosity and the willingness to try. This website is here to walk you through every step, from choosing your first project to making that final stitch. Take it one step at a time, be patient with yourself, and most importantly — have fun with it. You've got this!
