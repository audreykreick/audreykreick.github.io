Beginner Projects

Every quilter starts somewhere, and the best way to build your skills is by diving into a project! The patterns and tutorials below have been gathered from across the web with beginners in mind. These projects include simple shapes, straightforward instructions, and plenty of room to practice the basics. Whether you want to start small with a pot holder or pillowcase, or jump straight into your first full quilt, you'll find something here to get your needle and thread moving. Click any project to head to the original creator's page, and remember: your first project doesn't have to be perfect, just have fun!

Quilts are made for many different reasons. As beautiful, sturdy pieces, they are wonderful as decorative or useful items. In addition to being functional, creating one can be a fun project or an excellent way to strengthen sewing skills. Before creating a large quilt, starting with a smaller project can allow mistakes to be made with lower stakes involved. Creating a wall hanging is a great project for beginners. This can be made with one quilt block and can fill up empty wall space.

Another small, but slightly more difficult project could be a pillow. Two small quilt blocks sewn together and stuffed can add the perfect touch to couches, chairs, or beds. For those looking for a larger project but aren’t quite ready for a blanket, a table runner is great decor and serves as more practice before starting a big project. Sewers can even experiment with different holiday fabrics to make seasonal decor that can be passed down for generations.

Sewing a quilt blanket can be intimidating at first, but once the process is complete, it is very rewarding. The main difference in a larger quilt compared to the other projects is that there are more quilt blocks to create and sew together. While sewing, it is important to double-check the fabric before sewing it together, iron fabric pieces, and not to rush to ensure the final product displays the time spent on it.
