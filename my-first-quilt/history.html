How Quilting Began

Quilting is so much more than a craft — it's a tradition that stretches back centuries and spans cultures all around the world. Quilting has always been a way for people to tell stories, preserve memories, and pass something meaningful down through generations. Before you stitch your very first seam, take a moment to appreciate the rich history behind the art you're about to learn. You might just find that understanding where quilting comes from makes every project feel a little more special.
