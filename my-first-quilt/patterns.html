Find Patterns For Your First Project

One of the most exciting parts of quilting is choosing your pattern — it's where your project really starts to take shape! There are thousands of quilt patterns out there, ranging from simple beginner-friendly designs to intricate works of art, and the hardest part is often just picking one. Below you'll find a curated collection of patterns and resources gathered from some of the best quilting websites on the internet, all chosen with beginners in mind. Whether you're drawn to classic designs like the nine-patch or log cabin, or you're looking for something a little more modern, there's something here for everyone. Click any link to head to the original source and find the full pattern.
