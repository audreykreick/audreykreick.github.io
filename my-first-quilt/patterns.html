Find Patterns For Your First Project

One of the most exciting parts of quilting is choosing your pattern! It's where your project really starts to take shape! There are thousands of quilt patterns out there, ranging from simple beginner-friendly designs to intricate works of art. These can be found through various sources, such as online websites, books, or by creating one. By finding patterns online or in books, instructions will also be provided. They will provide information on sizes to cut the fabric, how to sew each piece together, and other necessary instructions to complete that pattern. After solidifying a pattern, fabric can be selected. Usually, a pattern is chosen before the fabric to ensure the best fabric is selected to match the desired outcome.

Below you'll find a curated collection of patterns and resources gathered from various quilting websites on the internet, all chosen with beginners in mind. Whether you're drawn to classic designs or you're looking for something a little more modern, there's something here for everyone. Click any pattern photo to head to the original source and find the full pattern.
