Pattern Inspiration

Figuring out what kind of fabric you should use for your quilt pattern can be a fun, but stressful process. My biggest piece of advice is to have a main fabric, then complementary fabrics with the same colors. You may have to look closely to see all the colors in the detailed patterns. You should also not have too many patterned fabrics in one quilt. Some less detailed and/ or solid colored fabrics will look best with your main one.
