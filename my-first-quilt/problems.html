How to Navigate Common Mistakes

Every quilter, no matter how experienced, has made a mistake. A seam that doesn't quite line up, a block that somehow came out the wrong size, or fabric that got cut just a little bit crooked. Mistakes are not a sign that you're doing it wrong, they're just part of the process! The good news is that most mistakes are totally fixable, and even the ones that aren't can usually be worked around in a way that nobody will ever notice. In this section, I'll share some of the most frequent beginner slip-ups, including plenty that I've made myself, and walk you through exactly how to handle them.
