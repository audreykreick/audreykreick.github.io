Create Your Own Pattern

Once you've gotten a few projects under your belt, you might start feeling the itch to create something that's entirely your own, and that's one of the most rewarding things you can do as a quilter! Designing your own pattern might sound intimidating at first, but it's more approachable than you think. In this section, I'll show you the basics of how quilt patterns work, how to plan your design, and how to figure out exactly how much fabric you'll need to bring your vision to life. Your pattern doesn't have to be complicated to be beautiful. Sometimes the simplest designs make the best quilts!
